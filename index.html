About

I'm a product designer. The Mac app started as a dumb question: can you use font ligatures to turn AIAI💩 into 💩? Turns out yes. Ironically, I used AIAI💩 to figure out how. The Chrome extension came after — web fonts don't always cooperate.

I don't dislike AIAI💩. I actually really like the technology and the things it's let me make. As with most fads, the problem is mostly the people, not the tech.

This is for fun. It's also a small ambient reminder of just how much AIAI💩 is being shoved into everything — as if you needed one.
